🛡️ Native System Antivirus (Samsung Smart Security) Official Recommendation

Due to Tizen TV's strict sandbox security model, third-party web apps cannot directly inspect kernel memory or scan other installed applications. Use Samsung's built-in Knox scanner to inspect the TV:

Step 1: Press Home on your remote and select Settings.
Step 2: Go to General (or General & Privacy) > System Manager.
Step 3: Select Smart Security > click Scan to scan for malicious code.
Step 4: Check the Isolated List to view quarantined threats or blocked network activity.
🌐 Clearing Malicious Browser Pop-Ups Common False-Virus Cause

Most "viruses" reported on Smart TVs are actually malicious browser cache entries or redirect scripts originating from websites visited in the TV Internet browser:

  • Open the Internet (Web Browser) app on the TV.
  • Open the top-right menu (⚙️ Settings) > Clear Browsing Data.
  • Clear Cookies, Cache, and Browsing History.
  • Enable Block Pop-ups in the browser security settings.
